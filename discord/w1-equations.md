## 🎯 What this covers
First order equations, and two ways through a system of two.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- ax + b = 0 gives x = −b/a. Everything else is getting the equation into that shape
- A system of two: eliminate a letter, or substitute one equation into the other
- Multiplying an equation means multiplying its right-hand side too
- Check your pair in both equations, not just one
