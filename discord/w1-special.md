## 🎯 What this covers
Recognising the family from the formula, and the shape that follows.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- Six families: constant, linear, quadratic, polynomial, rational and absolute value
- The family tells you the shape before you draw anything
- A case-defined function is several rules over several intervals
- Recognise the family from the formula, not from the picture
