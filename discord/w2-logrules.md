## 🎯 What this covers
Two tables of rules, the ones that do not exist, and what they are for.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- log(ab) = log a + log b, and log(a/b) = log a − log b
- log(aⁿ) = n log a, which is the rule that solves equations
- log(a + b) does not simplify. There is no such rule
- Change of base: log_b x = ln x / ln b
