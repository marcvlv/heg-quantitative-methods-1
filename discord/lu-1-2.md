## 🎯 What this covers
Terms, factors, and expanding a bracket without losing a sign.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- Distribute across every term in the bracket
- Two brackets: every term of the first against every term of the second
- Two patterns are worth knowing by sight
- A minus in front of a bracket reaches every term inside it
