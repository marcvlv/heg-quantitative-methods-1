## 🎯 What this covers
Where a curve meets each axis, and what that means in a business question.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- The y-intercept is f(0)
- The x-intercepts are the solutions of f(x) = 0
- In a business question an intercept is usually a fixed cost or a break-even point
- A vertical line is not the graph of a function
