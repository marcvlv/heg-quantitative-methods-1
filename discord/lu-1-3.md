## 🎯 What this covers
Writing an expression as a product, which is what lets you solve it.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- Take out the common factor first, every time
- For x² + bx + c, find two numbers that multiply to c and add to b
- Difference of two squares
- Perfect square
