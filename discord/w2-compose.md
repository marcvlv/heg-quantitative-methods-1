## 🎯 What this covers
Feeding one function’s output straight into the next.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- (f ∘ g)(x) means do g first, then f
- Order matters: f ∘ g is not g ∘ f
- The quantity in the middle is real, but the question is not about it
- For the composition to exist, g(x) has to be something f accepts
