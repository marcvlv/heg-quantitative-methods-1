## 🎯 What this covers
Five rules for exponents, and what a root is once you write it as one.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- Same base, multiplied: add the exponents
- Same base, divided: subtract them
- A power of a power: multiply them
- A product inside a power: the power reaches every factor
