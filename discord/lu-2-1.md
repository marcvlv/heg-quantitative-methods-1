## 🎯 What this covers
One unknown, first power, and the order of operations that clears it fastest.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- Whatever you do, do it to both sides
- Clear brackets first
- Then clear fractions by multiplying every term by the common denominator
- Collect the unknown on one side and the numbers on the other
