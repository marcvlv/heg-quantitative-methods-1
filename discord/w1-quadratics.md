## 🎯 What this covers
The discriminant, the formula, and the break-even question they answer.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- Everything to one side first: ax² + bx + c = 0
- Δ = b² − 4ac tells you how many roots before you find them
- Factoring is faster than the formula when the numbers are kind
- Break-even is where revenue equals cost, so it is a quadratic you already know how to solve
