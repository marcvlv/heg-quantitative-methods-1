## 🎯 What this covers
Which inputs are allowed, and which outputs actually occur.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- The domain is every input the rule accepts
- Three things are forbidden: a zero denominator, a negative under an even root, and a non-positive inside a log
- The range is what actually comes out, which is usually smaller than you expect
- Both can be read straight off a graph
