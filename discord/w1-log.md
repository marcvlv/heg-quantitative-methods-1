## 🎯 What this covers
The inverse of the exponential, and the two keys on your calculator.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- log_b x = y means exactly the same thing as bʸ = x
- It is the inverse of the exponential, so each undoes the other
- Two keys on the calculator: log is base 10, ln is base e
- Reading a statement in the other form is most of the work
