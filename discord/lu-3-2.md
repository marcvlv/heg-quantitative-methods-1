## 🎯 What this covers
One sign that flips, and the multiplier that makes percentages easy.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- An inequality solves like an equation, with one exception
- Multiplying or dividing by a negative flips the sign
- A double inequality: do the same thing to all three parts
- A percentage is a multiplier. That is the whole idea
