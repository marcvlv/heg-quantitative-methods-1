## 🎯 What this covers
One bar, four operations, and the cancellation that decides most of the marks.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- Multiply straight across. No preparation needed
- Divide by turning the second one over
- Only adding and subtracting need a common denominator
- Cancel factors, never terms
