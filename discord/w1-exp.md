## 🎯 What this covers
The variable moves into the exponent, and money is the reason.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- The variable sits in the exponent: f(x) = bˣ, with b > 0 and b ≠ 1
- Growth when b > 1, decay when b is between 0 and 1
- Compound interest is why it matters: S = P(1 + r)ⁿ
- e comes from a limit, and exp(x) is the exponential function
