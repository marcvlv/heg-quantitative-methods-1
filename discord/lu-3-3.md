## 🎯 What this covers
Two unknowns, two equations, and the two ways through.

## 🔑 Key takeaways
- Two unknowns need two independent equations
- Substitution: make one equation say x = something, then put that into the other
- Elimination: scale one or both so a letter cancels when you add or subtract
- If the letters vanish and a false statement is left, there is no solution
